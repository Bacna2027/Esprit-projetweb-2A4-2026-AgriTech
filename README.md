# AgriTech 

## Overview

AgriTech is a web platform that aims to make agriculture smarter and easier through technology. It helps farmers manage their agricultural activities and use resources more efficiently.

Developed at Esprit School of Engineering – Tunisia  
Academic Year: 2026–2027

## Features

Our platform includes five main modules:

- **Drone Management:** Manage agricultural drones and their missions.
- **Robot Management:** Manage agricultural robots and their interventions.
- **Energy Management:** Manage solar installations and monitor energy production and consumption.
- **Plant and Soil Management:** Manage plants and identify plants adapted to different soil types.
- **Climate Needs Management:** Manage the climate and lighting needs of plants.

## Tech Stack

## Architecture

AgriTech is organized into five modules. Each module focuses on a specific part of agricultural management, bringing everything together in one platform.
